### 1. Services Provided
Gaurav Autocare provides an automated WhatsApp ordering service for fuel truck orders (Petrol and Diesel) for vehicles, commercial trucks, and transport fleets.

---

### 2. User Responsibilities
- You agree to provide accurate vehicle registration numbers and requested fuel volumes.
- Orders submitted through the WhatsApp flow represent a binding commercial fuel request subject to confirmation by Gaurav Autocare dispatch desk.

---

### 3. Pricing and Payment
- Fuel rates for Petrol and Diesel are based on current daily pump prices at the time of delivery.
- Payments must be settled in accordance with agreed terms (cash, digital UPI, or authorized credit account).

---

### 4. Cancellation & Changes
- To modify or cancel an order, contact the dispatch desk immediately through WhatsApp or call our support line before the fuel truck departs.
