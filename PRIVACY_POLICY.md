# Privacy Policy for Gaurav Autocare WhatsApp Bot

**Effective Date:** October 28, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 28, 2026  

Welcome to **Gaurav Autocare** ("we", "our", or "us"). We operate an automated WhatsApp ordering system for fuel truck orders. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our WhatsApp bot.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
When you interact with our WhatsApp bot, we collect only the necessary information to process your fuel order:
- **Contact Details**: WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp profile name.
- **Order Details**: Vehicle registration number, selected fuel type (Petrol/Diesel), fuel quantity (litres), and estimated order amount.
- **Conversation Logs**: Timestamp of messages and state of your ordering session.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information exclusively to:
- Confirm and process your fuel truck delivery requests.
- Generate digital order tickets and send confirmation receipts via WhatsApp.
- Coordinate fuel dispensing and logistics with our dispatch team.
- Maintain customer order records and support customer inquiries.

We do **NOT** sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party marketers or advertisers.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Security
- All order and session information is securely stored in a cloud database (Supabase) protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies and encrypted connections (HTTPS / SSL).
- Only authorized dispatch and administrative personnel have access to order records.

---

### 4. User Data Deletion Instructions
You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data at any time:
1. Send an email to **admin@gauravauto.com** with the subject line: `"Data Deletion Request"`.
2. Include your registered WhatsApp phone number.
3. Upon receiving your request, our team will purge all personal data and order records associated with your phone number from our active databases within 48 business hours.
