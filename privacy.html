Gaurav Autocare — Privacy Policy

Official Policy Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Welcome to Gaurav Autocare. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, and process your information when you interact with our automated WhatsApp ordering service for fuel truck orders.

1. Information We Collect

When you message our WhatsApp bot or submit an order, we collect:

  • Contact Information: Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • Order Specifications: Vehicle registration number, selected fuel type (Petrol or Diesel), volume in litres, and estimated order amount.
  • Conversation Metadata: Message timestamps and current order session state.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for commercial fuel fulfillment:

  • To confirm and schedule fuel truck delivery for your registered vehicles.
  • To generate and issue digital order confirmation tickets via WhatsApp.
  • To allow our dispatch operations team to coordinate fueling logistics.

We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal data to any external advertising or marketing platforms.

3. Data Storage & Security

All order tickets and customer details are stored securely in cloud-hosted databases protected by industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) and strict access controls. Only authorized operational staff have access to order records.

4. User Data Deletion Instructions

You may request the deletion of your customer profile and WhatsApp chat records at any time. Simply send an email to admin@gauravauto.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your registered WhatsApp phone number. All records will be permanently removed within 48 business hours.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, please reach out to us:

  • Support Email: admin@gauravauto.com
  • Service: Fuel Truck Delivery Desk
